IDEA TITLE: GramArogya Proposed Solution
Team: Innovexa

Proposed Solution (Idea / Solution / Prototype)

End-to-End Assisted Rural Telehealth & Public Health Continuum

📌 1. Detailed Explanation of Proposed Solution
  • Care Continuum Framework: Connects citizens across 4 public tiers: Sub-Centres (Arogya Mandir) ➔ PHCs ➔ Rural Hospitals ➔ District Hospitals.
  • Assisted Teleconsultation: Low-bandwidth 2G video link connecting frontline ASHA workers + rural patients with remote specialist doctors.
  • Digital Triage & Vitals Pad: Real-time IoT vitals (BP, SpO2, Pulse, Sugar) & AI risk stratification into Green, Yellow, Red.
  • Longitudinal EHR: ABHA/ABDM compliant health locker maintaining complete patient history without information loss.
🎯 2. How it Addresses the Core Problem
  • Overcomes Travel Barriers: Eliminates 40+ km travel to cities for specialist opinions.
  • Slashes OPD Waiting Times: Digital token queue reduces wait time from 42 mins to 12 mins (-68%).
  • Affordability via Jan Aushadhi: Integrated generic drug comparison saves 80%+ on out-of-pocket prescription expenses.
  • Longitudinal Tracking: Active follow-up of maternal severe anemia (Hb < 8 g/dL) and chronic hypertension/diabetes.
💡 3. Innovation & Uniqueness of the Solution
  • Dual-Engine OCR: Client-side Tesseract.js (offline) + Server Gemini 2.5 Flash Multimodal Vision for handwritten Indian doctor prescriptions.
  • Vernacular Voice AI: Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech in Marathi & Hindi for illiterate rural citizens.
  • 1-Tap 108 Emergency Escalation: AI critical triage auto-dispatches 108 Ambulance with live GPS tracking to tertiary ICUs.
⚡ 4. Working Prototype Highlights
  • Full-stack Next.js 16 + React 19 + PostgreSQL PWA architecture.
  • Live WebRTC consultation suite with digital NMC doctor signatures.
  • Interactive Jan Aushadhi Kendra GIS locator & stockout prevention tracker.
  • District health surveillance heatmap for early outbreak alerts.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY Practical Execution
Team: Innovexa

Feasibility Analysis, Challenges & Mitigation Strategies

Ensuring Practical Field Deployment in Remote Maharashtra Villages

✅ Feasibility & Viability Analysis
  • Zero Hardware Cost: Runs on existing basic Android smartphones of ASHA/ANM workers via Progressive Web App (PWA).
  • Public System Complementarity: Strengthens rather than replaces existing public health facilities (Sub-Centres, PHCs, RH, DH).
  • ABDM Interoperability: Compliant with National Digital Health Mission (ABHA IDs) and FHIR data standards.
  • Scalability: Serverless PostgreSQL & edge functions scale easily to thousands of PHCs across all 36 districts of Maharashtra.
⚠️ Potential Challenges & Risks
  • Low/Zero Connectivity: Remote tribal and hilly regions face spotty 2G/3G connectivity.
  • Digital Literacy: Elderly patients and frontline health workers may find complex digital interfaces intimidating.
  • Handwritten Rx Variability: Severe doctor handwriting variations could cause OCR misinterpretations.
  • Doctor Workload: Shortage of medical officers at PHCs causing teleconsultation delays.
🛡️ Actionable Mitigation Strategies
  • Offline-First Architecture: Full offline local queue; records sync silently in background when internet resumes.
  • Vernacular Voice & Assisted Flow: ASHA worker acts as navigator; Marathi voice dictation eliminates typing.
  • Doctor-in-the-Loop Validation: AI extract requires mandatory doctor review before storing; 100% clinician authority.
  • Smart Triage Stratification: AI filters out routine cases, routing only Yellow/Red priorities to specialists.
